## Chapter overview
Moving up in any discipline should mean showing a real ability, not just finishing a number of weeks. These six simple field tests give you an honest starting point. They need little more than a bar and a stopwatch, and all are optional.
Levlofit's own level checks are set per goal in the app. The tests here are for tracking yourself, and they do not set your level there.

## What a field test can tell you
> evidence: Evidence: moderate, with an important limit
> One study of field tests including pull-ups and push-ups found they were not valid measures of absolute strength, but were valid for strength relative to body weight. The study was in 94 children aged 9 to 10, so it applies to adults only by reasoning. {pate1993}
In practice, a push-up or pull-up count is a repeatable way to compare yourself with yourself over months. Do not treat it as a stand-in for a one-repetition maximum.

## The six tests
Warm up first, and stop when your form breaks. These are practical test descriptions.
- **Strict push-ups.** Full range, chest near the floor, no sagging hips, until form breaks.
- **Strict pull-ups or chin-ups.** Start from a dead hang, chin clears the bar, no swinging.
- **Dead hang.** Time how long your grip holds.
- **Plank or hollow-body hold.** Time until the position collapses.
- **1 km run.** Flat ground, at your own pace, timed.
- **Standing broad jump.** Take off and land on two feet. Measure to your heels. Take your best of three.
> warn: These are maximal efforts
> If you have a joint, tendon or heart condition that a hard test could aggravate, skip that test and ask your doctor first. Stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness. A partial baseline is still useful.

## Retesting
Retest every 8 to 12 weeks if you want a check, at the start of a new block rather than at the end of a tiring one. This interval is practical guidance. A result that has not moved is information, not failure. Change one thing at a time.

## Key takeaways
- These tests measure how well you move your own body.
- They are good for comparing yourself over time, and poor stand-ins for maximum strength.
- The main study behind this was in children, so treat its findings as a guide.
- All of them are optional. Skip any your health makes unwise.
