## Chapter overview
Levlofit is built level first. You train your main goal until the basics are solid, then new goals unlock.

## The Levlofit ladder
| Level | Name | What it means |
| 0 | Starting out | Learn the moves, build the habit. |
| 1 | Foundation | Basic strength and fitness in place. |
| 2 | Building | Steady progress on your main goal. |
| 3 | Solid | A good level for most people. |
| 4 | Advanced | Specialised training pays off. |
| 5 | Expert | Coach-led territory. |
You move up in four steps: train the plan, log your sets, take the level check, and unlock the next level.

## What decides your plan
- **Your answers.** Health check, body numbers, days, time and equipment.
- **Your level.** From the level check in Workout.
- **Rules, not AI.** Numbers come from cited rules. Levlofit AI explains them and never invents them.
> tip: Levlofit rule
> Levlofit builds the basics before adding goals, so your training stays focused while you learn and recover.

## What you can change
| Change anytime in Protocol | Earned, not edited |
| Goals and main goal | Your level |
| Training days and time | When new goals unlock |
| Diet, allergies and budget | |

## Key takeaways
- Levels run from 0 to 5 in each goal and are earned with level checks.
- Your answers, your level and cited rules decide your plan. Levlofit AI explains, and does not invent numbers.
- Goals, days and diet can change anytime in Protocol. Levels are earned.
