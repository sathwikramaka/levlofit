## Chapter overview
This closing chapter walks through how to put training into a week, then answers some common questions. It is practical guidance built on the rest of this Handbook. Your Levlofit plan does this for you, and this chapter helps you understand and adjust it.

## Building a week, in order
- **Start with your main goal.** It has first claim on your days.
- **Add other goals at a smaller, steady dose.** See [[combining-training|Combining different kinds of training]].
- **Fit it to your equipment.** Use what you actually have.
- **Respect your limits.** Remove anything that aggravates a known problem. If your session is short, trim small exercises first.
- **Set your starting point honestly.** Start from where you actually are.
For health, aim for WHO's guidelines as well: 150 to 300 minutes of moderate activity a week, or 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous activity, or a mix, along with muscle strengthening. {who2020}

## The long view
No one calendar suits everyone. A useful pattern is to build the basics before specialising, check your progress at the end of each block, and reduce your training when fatigue says so. A year of training is that cycle repeated. Your goals and equipment can change between cycles.

## Troubleshooting
| Question | Answer |
| My extra goal has no room this week. | You may not have enough days for both. Keep your main goal, trim the extra one, or add a day if you can recover from it. |
| My skill is stuck on one step. | Skills move up when you can do the next step with control, not because time has passed. Practise, and retest. |
| My plan has a different exercise from what I expected. | You may not have the equipment, or a limit you told Levlofit about may rule it out. Check your equipment and limits, or ask for a swap. |
| Can I add sparring or partner drills? | Do this only with a qualified coach in person. See [[boxing-solo|Boxing: solo training]]. |
| I keep missing sessions. | Choose fewer days you can actually keep. Stress and busy periods often show up as missed sessions. {stults2014} |
| Everything is falling at once. | Reduce your training, and check sleep and food. See [[plateaus|Breaking through plateaus]]. |
| Something hurts. | Stop that exercise. See [[injury-prevention|Injury prevention]]. |

## Key takeaways
- Start from your main goal, your days and your equipment.
- Build the basics before specialising.
- Review at the end of each block, and reduce when fatigue says so.
- Consistency over years beats perfection over weeks.
