## Chapter overview
One kettlebell can build strength and power: six weeks of twice-weekly swings improved both in a trial. {lake2012} The swing is the centre of it all.

## The main moves
| Move | Trains | Start with |
| Swing | Hips, back, power, heart | 3 x 10 |
| Goblet squat | Legs, trunk | 3 x 8 |
| Press | Shoulders | 3 x 5 each arm |
| Row | Back, grip | 3 x 8 each arm |
| Farmers carry | Grip, trunk | 3 x 30 m |

## The swing
- **Hinge.** Hips back, flat back, bell between the forearms.
- **Snap.** Drive the hips forward hard, glutes tight.
- **Float.** Arms only guide; the bell rises to chest height.
- **Return.** Let it fall, then hinge again.

## What the research shows
> evidence: Evidence: moderate
> Reviews find kettlebell training improves strength and fitness, but most studies are small. {meigh2019}

## Key takeaways
- The swing is a hip hinge: snap the hips, let the arms guide.
- One bell trains strength, power and conditioning.
- Reviews find benefits, but most studies are small.
