## Chapter overview
You do not need to weigh every meal to eat well. A plate split into the right parts gets most meals close: half vegetables and fruit, a quarter grains, a quarter protein, a little healthy fat, and water to drink. The idea comes from Harvard's Healthy Eating Plate {harvardplate}; the amounts below come from ICMR-NIN's guidelines for Indian diets. {icmrdg2024}

## The plate
:: plate One meal on the plate | {harvardplate}
Vegetables (more than fruit; potatoes do not count) | 0.4 | sabzi, salad, dal-vegetable mixes, greens
Fruit | 0.1 | whole fruit rather than juice
Grains, mostly whole | 0.25 | roti, brown or hand-pounded rice, millets, oats
Protein | 0.25 | dal, paneer, curd, eggs, chicken, fish, soya, chana
::
Add a small amount of healthy oil for cooking (groundnut, mustard, sesame, rice bran), and drink water, buttermilk, or tea and coffee with little or no sugar. {harvardplate}

## A day on ICMR's plate
:: bars A day's food for about 2,000 kcal | g or ml a day | {icmrdg2024}
Vegetables, including leafy greens | 400 | half the plate across the day
Cereals and millets (raw weight) | 250 | rice, wheat, millets
Milk and curd | 300 | ml
Fruit | 100 | one medium fruit
Pulses, eggs, meat or fish | 85 | raw weight; more for higher protein targets
Nuts and seeds | 35 | a small handful
Fats and oils | 27 | about 5 to 6 teaspoons, all cooking included
::
These are ICMR-NIN's amounts for a moderately active adult. {icmrdg2024} Your Levlofit plan sets your own calories and protein, so your portions may be bigger or smaller; the shares stay similar. People who train hard usually need more protein than this day gives, which is why your plan adds protein foods. See [[protein|Protein]].

## Fixing common plates
:: compare Common plate | Better plate
Three rotis, a little sabzi, no protein | Two rotis, a full bowl of dal or paneer sabzi, salad
Big mound of rice, thin dal | Half the rice, a thick bowl of dal or rajma, a vegetable side
Poha or upma alone for breakfast | Poha with peanuts and a glass of milk or two eggs
Biryani with raita | Smaller biryani, a bigger raita, a salad, chicken or paneer pieces
Fruit juice with snacks | Whole fruit, and water or buttermilk to drink
::

## Eating well without counting
:: steps Simple rules that work
Build every main meal on the plate | Half vegetables and fruit, a quarter grains, a quarter protein.
Put protein in every meal | Dal, curd, paneer, eggs, chicken, fish or soya, every time.
Prefer whole foods | Whole grains, whole fruit, home-cooked over packaged most days.
Limit the extras, do not ban them | Sweets, fried snacks, sugary drinks and alcohol in small, planned amounts.
Keep snacking deliberate | If losing weight, snack only if it is planned in your day.
::
No single food makes or breaks a diet. "Superfoods" are marketing; what matters is the total over your days and weeks. A meal plan is good if it meets your calories and protein, has plenty of vegetables and fruit, and is one you will keep eating. See [[nutrition-fundamentals|Nutrition fundamentals]].

## A lifestyle, not a diet
Diets you plan to "come off" usually end with the weight coming back. Change your normal meals a little at a time instead: one more vegetable, one more protein serving, one less sugary drink. Your favourite foods can stay; the portions and how often change. See [[sustainable-habits|Building habits that last]].

## Estimating food you did not make
:: steps At a restaurant, a canteen or a friend's house
Use your hand | A palm of protein, a fist of rice or grains, a cupped hand of snacks, a thumb of oil or ghee.
Compare to what you know | A restaurant curry bowl often holds 2 to 3 home servings, with more oil.
Log the closest match | Pick the nearest food in the diary; close is better than skipping.
Learn from repeats | Weigh a home serving of foods you eat often once, so your eye learns.
::
See [[eating-out|Eating out, travel and occasions]].

## Key takeaways
- Half vegetables and fruit, a quarter grains, a quarter protein, a little healthy oil, water to drink.
- ICMR's day for 2,000 kcal: 400 g vegetables, 250 g cereals, 300 ml milk, 100 g fruit, 85 g pulses or meat, 35 g nuts, 27 g oil.
- Protein in every meal, extras planned not banned, and changes you can keep.
