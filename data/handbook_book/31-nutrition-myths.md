## Chapter overview
This chapter gathers the volume's corrections. Each answer says what supports it. Where the evidence is thin or divided, it says so.

## Energy and macronutrients
| Claim | Answer |
| "Carbohydrates make you fat." | False. Fat loss is driven by a calorie deficit, and a wide range of diets can work. {aragon2017} |
| "Eating after a certain hour causes fat gain." | Not supported as a rule. Your total intake over weeks decides your direction. {aragon2017} |
| "You must avoid sugar completely." | Overstated. Limiting added sugar is sensible, but you do not need to eliminate it. |
| "One cheat meal ruins everything." | False. One meal is small against a month. |
| "You can lose fat from one chosen area." | Do not rely on it. Fat loss in an area generally follows overall fat loss. |

## Protein
| Claim | Answer |
| "The body can only use 30 g of protein per meal." | Not what the ISSN says. It gives 20 to 40 g as a general dose to stimulate muscle protein synthesis, and says the findings are mixed. It does not set that as a limit on what you can use. {issnprotein} |
| "High protein damages your kidneys." | If you have kidney disease, your protein is a matter for your doctor. Levlofit does not advise on it. |
| "Vegetarians cannot build muscle." | Not supported. Plant protein research is limited, but rice protein matched whey in one 8-week study. {issnprotein} |
| "Protein powder is required." | False. It is a practical option, and needs can be met from food. {issnprotein} |
| "More protein is always better." | Not shown. 1.4 to 2.0 g per kg is sufficient for most exercising people. Evidence above 3.0 g per kg is novel. {issnprotein} |

## Timing
| Claim | Answer |
| "You must eat within 30 minutes of training." | False. The effect of exercise lasts at least 24 hours, and eating before or after both help. {issnprotein} |
| "You must eat every two to three hours." | Partly. The number of meals has limited effect on weight loss and body composition when not exercising. {kerksick2017} Spreading protein every 3 to 4 hours is the stated ideal. {issnprotein} |
| "Training without eating burns more fat, or ruins muscle." | Treat it as a scheduling choice. Eat first if you feel weak or light-headed. |
| "You need carbohydrate during every workout." | Depends. Timed carbohydrate matters less unless you need rapid recovery. {kerksick2017} |

## Supplements
| Claim | Answer |
| "Creatine damages your kidneys." | Not supported in healthy people, including up to 30 g a day for 5 years. Kidney disease is a separate matter for your doctor. {issncreatine} |
| "Creatine causes cramps and dehydration." | Not supported. Athlete studies reported no effect or less of both. {issncreatine} |
| "You must load creatine." | False. Loading is faster, and a daily dose gets there more slowly. {issncreatine} |
| "Caffeine is a proven lifting supplement." | Overstated. Endurance shows the most consistent benefit. Benefits for strength are small to moderate. {issncaffeine} |
| "Fat burners melt fat." | False. Fat loss comes from a calorie deficit. {aragon2017} |
| "BCAAs help if you already eat enough protein." | Little reason to expect it. Protein already contains them. |
| "Natural means safe." | False. Plant extracts can be active and can interact with medicines. |

## Hydration
| Claim | Answer |
| "Everyone should drink a fixed amount of water a day." | No one number fits everyone. Treat your water goal as a reminder. |
| "Waiting until you are thirsty is always a mistake." | False for shorter, cooler or lighter sessions. It falls short when you sweat heavily. {kenefick2018} |
| "More water is always safer." | False. Drinking too much can be dangerous. Never gain weight during exercise. {kenefick2018} |
| "Cramp means you need electrolytes." | Contested. Frequent or severe cramps, or cramps at rest, need a clinician. |
| "You need a sports drink for a gym session." | Usually not. Water covers most ordinary sessions. |

## Common questions
| Question | Answer |
| How often should I weigh myself? | Most days, in the same conditions. Judge by your seven-day average. |
| Should I eat before the gym? | Often helpful, not required. Find what feels comfortable. |
| How do I know my maintenance calories? | Eat consistently for two to three weeks and compare weekly averages. |
| How fast should I lose fat? | About 0.5 to 1% of body weight a week is a reasonable pace, slower as you get leaner. {helms2014} |
| Can I gain muscle and lose fat together? | Often, especially if you are new, returning or carrying more body fat. No reliable rate is established. |
| Do I need supplements? | Mostly no. Creatine is worth considering, and caffeine depends on you. |
| Is a vegetarian or vegan diet a disadvantage? | It can work with more attention to protein and variety. On a fully plant-based diet, B12 needs a supplement or fortified food. |
| Should I get a blood test before taking vitamins or minerals? | Yes, then have a clinician read it. Some are harmful in excess. |
| My weight jumped 2 kg overnight. What happened? | Most likely water, food, salt, carbohydrate or digestion. Your seven-day average will smooth it out. |
| I have a medical condition. Does this apply to me? | Treat it as general education only, and get advice from a qualified clinician or dietitian. |

## The volume, in brief
- Energy sets your direction. Protein helps decide how much is muscle. Sticking with it decides whether either happens.
- Aim for about 1.4 to 2.0 g of protein per kg a day, across your meals. {issnprotein}
- A wide range of diets can work. Use one you will follow. {aragon2017}
- Timing is a refinement.
- Creatine, and possibly caffeine. Very little else.
- Judge progress by your seven-day average, over months.

## Daily checklist
- Did each main meal have a protein source?
- Was my food about right for my current goal?
- Did I include vegetables and fruit?
- Did I drink across the day, and more in the heat?
- Did I ignore any one day's number on the scale?

## Key takeaways
- Many nutrition arguments are about things that barely matter.
- Check what supports a claim before you act on it, including the claims in this Handbook.
- Simple habits kept for years beat perfect ones kept for weeks.
