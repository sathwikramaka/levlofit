## Chapter overview
This book explains the why behind your Levlofit plan. Read the opening chapters in order, then come back to any chapter when a question comes up.

## The six volumes
| Volume | What you will learn |
| I. Training | Starting safely, how your plan is built, how training works, technique, warming up, sessions, lighter weeks, plateaus and injury prevention |
| II. Nutrition | The basics, protein, vegetarian eating, calories, gaining weight, eating too little, timing, budget, supplements, water and eating out |
| III. Recovery | Sleep, stress, mobility, soreness, motivation, habits and daily steps |
| IV. Physique | Body fat, proportions, lagging muscles and posture |
| V. Tracking | Photos, measurements, body weight, your workout log, reviews and goals |
| VI. Disciplines | General fitness, strength, calisthenics, running, HYROX, boxing, kettlebells, home training, training as a woman and training after 40 |

## How to read a chapter
- **Chapter overview.** Each chapter opens with its main idea.
- **Evidence notes.** These say how strong the research is. [[foundations|Foundations]] explains the grades.
- **Levlofit rules.** A number Levlofit uses where research gives no exact figure. Treat it as a sensible starting point, not a research finding.
- **Source marks.** Numbered marks point to the full list at the end of each chapter.
- **Key takeaways.** Each chapter ends with a short summary.

## Words used in this book
| Word | Meaning in this book |
| Session | One training appointment, from warm-up to the last set |
| Set and rep | A rep is one lift; a set is a group of reps done without rest |
| RIR | Reps in reserve: how many more good reps you could have done |
| Deload | A planned lighter week to shed fatigue |
| Maintenance | Eating about as much energy as you burn |
| Level | Your stage, 0 to 5, in each goal; levels are earned with a level check |
| Protocol | The app screen where you change your goals, training days and diet |
| Workout | The app tab with your sessions and level checks |

## Who this book is for
| This book is | This book is not |
| For healthy adults aged 18 and over; Levlofit plans cover ages 18 to 80 | Medical advice, or a replacement for your doctor, physiotherapist or dietitian |
| Built on cited guidelines and research, with numbers named to their source | Written for children or teenagers, who have their own activity guidelines |
| A companion to your plan in the app | A plan for pregnancy or a medical condition without your doctor's input |
