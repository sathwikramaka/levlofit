## Chapter overview
Muscle and balance fade with age unless you train them. It is never too late: older adults gain strength well.

## What the research says
> evidence: Evidence: strong
> WHO advises older adults to do varied balance and strength work on 3 or more days a week to prevent falls. {who2020}
Older adults need more training than younger adults to keep muscle size. {bickel2011}

## Smart changes
- **Longer warm-up.** 10 minutes, gradually heavier.
- **Moderate reps.** 8 to 15 reps with good control.
- **Balance every week.** Single-leg stands, step-ups, heel-to-toe walking.
- **Recover.** Levlofit rule: 48 hours between hard sessions for the same muscles.
> warn: Check first
> Levlofit builds plans for ages 18 to 80. Over 65, or living with a long-term condition? Read [[safety-first|Safe to start]] again and check with your doctor before hard training.

## Key takeaways
- Strength and balance fade with age unless you train them. It is never too late.
- Older adults: varied balance and strength work on 3 or more days a week.
- Warm up longer and leave 48 hours between hard sessions for the same muscles.
