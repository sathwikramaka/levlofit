## Chapter overview
This chapter adds no new evidence. It closes the volume where this Handbook most often has to say "this is not science".
In brief: muscle and body fat are the two things you can train here, and you cannot control where fat comes off. Proportion, taper, symmetry and posture correction are convention, reasoning or association.

## Myths
| Claim | What this volume found |
| "You can burn fat from one area." | Do not rely on it. Overall fat loss is what matters. |
| "This exercise builds the upper chest or outer thigh." | Thin evidence. One lower-body study. {burke2025} |
| "Slow, controlled reps build more muscle." | Growth was similar from 0.5 to 8 seconds per repetition. {schoenfeld2015} |
| "Bad posture causes back pain." | No consensus on cause. {swain2020} |
| "Forward head posture causes neck pain." | Linked in adults, not in adolescents, and cause is not shown. {mahmoud2019} |
| "Training your core thickens your waist." | Not established. |
| "Training widens your shoulders." | Muscle over the frame grows. Adult bone width does not change with training. |
| "A body-fat scale tells you your body fat." | It gives an estimate, and it tracked 12-week change poorly against DEXA. {merrigan2022} |
| "Sweating more burns more fat." | Sweat is mostly fluid loss. Fat loss comes from a calorie deficit. {aragon2017} |
| "Lifting will make you bulky by accident." | Muscle grows slowly, so this is unlikely. |
| "No pain, no gain." | Soreness mostly reflects unfamiliar work. |

## Common questions
| Question | Answer |
| How long does an aesthetic physique take? | Usually years. Your log will often show change before a photo does. |
| Should I cut or bulk first? | This is practice, not a trial result. A larger deficit may suit those with more body fat, and slower loss helps leaner people keep lean mass. {aragon2017} |
| Can I build muscle and lose fat at once? | Often, especially if you are new, returning or carrying more body fat. No reliable rate is established. |
| What about supplements for physique? | See [[supplements-guide|Supplements]]. Creatine is the main one with strong support. {issncreatine} |
| How often should I change my plan? | Less often than most people do. Periodised plans were not found better for growth. {acsm2026} |
| Do I need good genetics? | Genes influence your frame and how you respond. Training decides what you do with them. That is a reason to stop comparing, not to stop training. |

## How firm each finding is
| Finding | Grade |
| Resistance training helps preserve muscle in a deficit | Strong, position stand. {aragon2017} |
| More protein may help lean lifters in a deficit | Position stand, "may be required". {aragon2017} |
| Sticking with it decides long-term results | Position stand. {aragon2017} |
| More weekly sets grow more muscle, with shrinking returns | Moderate. {pelland2025} |
| Repetition length from 0.5 to 8 seconds does not change growth | Moderate. {schoenfeld2015} |
| Body-fat scanners are poor at tracking change | Moderate, one study. {merrigan2022} |
| Muscle dysmorphia is linked with steroid use and obsessive traits | Emerging. {cinaroglu2025} |
| Low energy availability harms health and performance in athletes | Consensus. {mountjoy2023} |
| Posture causes back pain | No consensus. {swain2020} |
| Exercises can sculpt one region | Thin. {burke2025} |
| Proportion, taper and symmetry as goals | Preference. |

## Red flags in physique content
- Dramatic change promised in weeks.
- A product that changes your body without changing your food or activity.
- Any claim to remove fat from one named area.
- A plan sold on sculpting regions.
- Correction programmes sold on posture causing pain.
- Before and after photos with different lighting, pose or timing.
- Treating a scale's body-fat figure as a measurement.
- Anyone offering guidance on anabolic steroids. Levlofit gives none.

## Key takeaways
- Muscle and body fat are the two things you can train here.
- Controlling where fat goes or how a muscle is shaped is a common claim with little support.
- Much of this field has not been tested, and honest labels matter.
- The safety warnings in this volume are there to be used.
