## Chapter overview
For lifelong health, do some of everything: lift twice a week, move your heart most days, and sit less.

## The targets
- 150 to 300 minutes of moderate cardio a week. {who2020}
- 2 days of strength training a week. {who2020}

## A balanced week
| Day | Session | Type |
| Mon | Full body | Strength |
| Tue | Brisk walk | Cardio |
| Wed | Cycle or swim | Cardio |
| Thu | Full body | Strength |
| Fri | Walk | Cardio |

| Sun | Rest | Rest |

## Why heart fitness matters
> evidence: Evidence: strong
> In 122,007 adults, low cardiorespiratory fitness carried a mortality risk as large as smoking or diabetes, with no upper limit of benefit. The risk of death was about 5 times higher with the lowest fitness than with the highest. {mandsager2018}
| Builds heart fitness | Easy to forget |
| Brisk walking, cycling, swimming | Long sitting without breaks |
| Sports and games | Only lifting, no cardio |
| Stairs instead of the lift | |

## Key takeaways
- Lift twice a week, move your heart most days and sit less.
- 150 to 300 minutes of moderate cardio a week is the target.
- Low fitness carries a large health risk, and higher fitness showed no upper limit of benefit.
