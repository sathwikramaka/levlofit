# Exercise animations: provenance

## CDC "Growing Stronger: Strength Training for Older Adults" (public domain)

- Work: animated GIFs from the Growing Stronger programme of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), made
  with Tufts University's John Hancock Center for Physical Activity and Nutrition.
- Rights evidence (checked 2026-10-27):
  - Each file's Wikimedia Commons page says "Public domain": "a work of the Centers for Disease Control and
    Prevention ... As a work of the U.S. federal government, the file is in the public domain."
  - The CDC Stacks record of the Growing Stronger publication (stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/11447) gives
    Rights: "Public Domain".
  - No copyright notice or illustrator credit was found.
- Residual risk: Tufts University co-authored the programme, and the original CDC page
  (cdc.gov/physicalactivity/growingstronger/exercises/stage1.html) now returns 404, so it could not be checked for a
  third-party credit. CDC's reuse page warns that some CDC images were made by contractors or licensed from third
  parties. If anyone claims rights, remove the entry from `moveAnimations`; the move falls back to its drawing or the
  icon.
- CDC's conditions for reusing public-domain material (cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html) and how Levlofit meets them:
  - Attribution to the agency: the credit line "Animations: CDC, Growing Stronger (public domain)" and the Licences
    page entry.
  - No implied endorsement: the Licences page states that CDC does not endorse Levlofit.
  - No substantive changes: the files are bundled unmodified (byte-identical; SHA-1 matches Commons) and only scaled.
  - Availability at no charge: the Licences page states that the material was published free of charge by CDC.
- Downloaded 2026-10-27 from upload.wikimedia.org, with the owner's approval. Each file's SHA-1 equals the hash
  Commons publishes for it.

- Chest_press: a floor press, not the bench press it would stand for.
