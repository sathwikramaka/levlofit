## Chapter overview
Eating too little for too long costs muscle, energy and health. Levlofit never sets your calories below your resting burn, but you are the first to notice when eating has become a struggle.

## What Levlofit does
- **Your resting burn (BMR) is the floor.** Levlofit never sets your calorie target below it.
- **No deficit when underweight.** When your BMI is below the healthy range, Levlofit keeps you at maintenance instead.

## The signs
| Signs to slow down the diet | Signs to talk to a doctor |
| Weight falling faster than your planned pace for two weeks or more | Periods stopping or becoming irregular |
| Strength dropping for several weeks in a row | Fainting or dizziness |
| Constant hunger, poor sleep or low energy | Feeling unable to stop restricting food, bingeing, making yourself sick, or exercising to make up for eating |
| | Thoughts about food, weight or your body taking over most of the day |
> evidence: Evidence: moderate
> In lean athletes, low energy availability is linked to lower testosterone in men and is the proposed cause of missed periods, called athletic amenorrhea, in women. {helms2014}

## Is dieting still right for you?
| Question | If yes | If no |
| Is your BMI below the healthy range? | Do not diet. Levlofit keeps you at maintenance and builds strength instead. | Go to the next question. |
| Does any sign in the doctor list apply to you? | Pause the fat-loss phase and talk to a doctor. | Carry on at the planned pace and review every 2 to 3 weeks. |

## Key takeaways
- Levlofit never sets calories below your BMR, and keeps underweight users at maintenance.
- Slow the diet if weight falls faster than planned or strength keeps dropping.
- Missed periods, fainting or a loss of control around food mean talk to a doctor.
