## Chapter overview
Much fitness content assumes a full gym and treats everything else as a compromise. Levlofit asks what you actually have, and builds your plan around that. This chapter explains the thinking, so you can make good swaps yourself.

## What drives results
The ACSM concludes that many forms of resistance training work, and calls for programmes to be fitted to the person. {acsm2026} A well-run bodyweight plan and a well-run barbell plan are both real training. A poor substitution, made without knowing it, is the real problem.

## What your equipment opens up
| You have | What it opens up |
| Bodyweight only | Bodyweight movements, including the [[calisthenics-ladders|calisthenics ladders]] |
| Dumbbells | Dumbbell and carry movements, plus bodyweight |
| Full gym | Barbells, cables, machines and cardio equipment, plus the above |
| Kettlebell | Kettlebell and carry work |
| Bands, suspension trainer or rings | Their own kinds of load |

## Fixtures matter too
Some movements need somewhere to hang or stand. A pull-up needs a bar, and a box jump needs a box. A doorway pull-up bar is one of the cheapest items that can open up a lot of pulling work. Fit it securely, follow its limits and test it before you trust your weight to it.

## When nothing fits
If a movement needs equipment you lack, use the swap test from [[push-sessions|Building a push session]]: same movement pattern, same joint action. If nothing matches, choose the closest option and be honest with yourself that it is a different exercise. You can tell Levlofit what you have, and ask it to swap an exercise.

## Limits of bodyweight training
- Adding load is harder, so use reps, slower progressions and harder positions.
- Using only body mass did not significantly improve range of motion in one review, so you may want separate mobility work. {alizadeh2023}
- Pulling work is hard to do without a bar. Inverted rows under a sturdy table can work if it will safely hold you, and a bar or bands are a good first purchase.

## Key takeaways
- Many forms of resistance training work.
- Use what you have, and swap honestly.
- A bar or bands open up a lot of pulling work.
- Bodyweight-only trainees may need separate mobility work.
