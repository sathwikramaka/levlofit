## Chapter overview
Protein is the one macronutrient with a clear daily number to aim for, which is why it gets a chapter of its own. It supplies the amino acids your body uses to repair and build muscle.
Protein does not build muscle on its own. Training asks your body to adapt, and protein is part of what pays for it.

## How much
> evidence: Evidence: position stand
> The International Society of Sports Nutrition states that **1.4 to 2.0 g of protein per kg of body weight per day is sufficient for most exercising people** for building and keeping muscle. {issnprotein}
> The same paper calls the evidence for intakes above 3.0 g per kg novel. It may help body composition in resistance-trained people, but it is not established. {issnprotein}
| Situation | Daily intake | Standing |
| Healthy, inactive Indian adult | 0.83 g per kg | The Indian RDA. It covers daily needs, not training. {icmr2020} |
| Training, building or keeping muscle | 1.4 to 2.0 g per kg | Position stand. {issnprotein} |
| Already lean, resistance training, eating below your needs | 2.3 to 3.1 g per kg of **fat-free** mass | "May be required", so not a firm rule. Fat-free mass is a smaller number than body weight. {aragon2017} |
These figures are per kg of body weight, except the last row. If you train and eat between about 1.4 and 2.0 g per kg, you are in the supported range.

## Per meal, and across the day
Your total for the day comes first. How you spread it is a smaller refinement.
| Question | What the position stand says |
| How much per meal? | About 0.25 g per kg of body weight, or 20 to 40 g. It says the findings are mixed and depend on age and recent training. {issnprotein} |
| How often? | Ideally spread evenly, every 3 to 4 hours. {issnprotein} |
| What makes a dose work? | All the essential amino acids, with 700 to 3000 mg of leucine. {issnprotein} |
| Before sleep? | 30 to 40 g of casein raises overnight muscle protein synthesis. {issnprotein} |
For most people this comes down to one habit: **have a real protein source at each main meal** instead of eating most of it at dinner. That helps both your daily total and how it is spread.

## Timing around training
You do not have to drink protein within minutes of finishing a workout.
> evidence: Evidence: position stand
> The best time is likely a matter of what you tolerate, since benefits come from eating before or after training. **The effect of exercise lasts at least 24 hours**, though it likely fades with time. {issnprotein}
> tip: In practice
> Eat a meal with protein within a few hours before or after training, then stop thinking about it.

## Sources
Proteins that digest quickly and are high in essential amino acids and leucine stimulate muscle protein synthesis most. The position stand suggests focusing on whole foods that contain all the essential amino acids. {issnprotein}
| Food, raw | Protein |
| Soya chunks, 50 g dry | 25.7 g {usda174275} |
| Paneer, 100 g | 18.9 g {ifct2017} |
| Chicken breast, skinless, 100 g | 21.8 g {ifct2017} |
| Masoor dal, 50 g dry | 12.2 g {ifct2017} |
| Kala chana, 50 g dry | 9.4 g {ifct2017} |
| Whole egg, 50 g without shell | 6.6 g {ifct2017} |
| Cow milk, 250 g | 8.2 g {ifct2017} |
These are for planning. Where a pack has a label, trust the label.
### Vegetarian and vegan diets
The position stand says research on plant proteins such as pea, rice and hemp is limited. In one study it reports, protein synthesis after resistance exercise was about 31% higher with whey than with soy. In another, 48 g a day of whey or rice protein gave similar gains over 8 weeks in experienced lifters. {issnprotein}

## Myths, corrected
| Claim | Standing |
| "Only animal protein builds muscle." | Not supported. Rice protein matched whey in the 8-week study above, though the research is limited. {issnprotein} |
| "More protein is always better." | Not shown. 1.4 to 2.0 g per kg is sufficient for most exercising people, and the evidence above 3.0 g per kg is still novel. {issnprotein} |
| "You need a shake straight after training." | False. The effect of exercise lasts at least 24 hours. {issnprotein} |
| "Protein powder is necessary." | False. The position stand calls it a practical option, and says daily needs can be met from whole foods. {issnprotein} |
> warn: If you have kidney disease
> Your doctor should set your protein intake. Levlofit does not advise on it.

## Practical application
- Your Levlofit plan sets a daily target. Judge it across the week, not one day at a time.
- Build each main meal around a protein source.
- If you are often short, add curd or milk at breakfast, dal or soya at one main meal, and eggs if you eat them.
- Use powder if it makes the target easier to reach. It is not a better food.

## Key takeaways
- 1.4 to 2.0 g per kg a day is sufficient for most exercising people.
- Lean people training on fewer calories may need more, counted per kg of fat-free mass.
- Your daily total matters most. Spreading it out is a smaller refinement.
- There is no narrow window after training. The effect lasts at least a day.
