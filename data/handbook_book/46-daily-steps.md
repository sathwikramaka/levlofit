## Chapter overview
Walking more is one of the simplest health wins, and you do not need 10,000 steps to get most of it.

## Where the benefit levels off
| Age | Steps a day |
| Adults under 60 | 8,000 to 10,000 |
| Adults 60 and over | 6,000 to 8,000 |
> evidence: Evidence: strong
> More daily steps went with lower risk, and the benefit levelled off at a lower count for older adults. Risk of death was 40 to 53% lower in the most active step groups compared with the least. {paluch2022}

## Add steps without trying
- **Walk after meals.** Ten minutes after lunch and dinner adds up fast.
- **Take calls standing.** Pace while you talk.
- **Use the stairs.** Skip the lift for a few floors.

## Key takeaways
- Walking more is one of the simplest health wins.
- The benefit levels off around 8,000 to 10,000 steps under 60, and 6,000 to 8,000 from 60.
- Walk after meals, take calls standing and use the stairs.
