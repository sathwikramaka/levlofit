## Chapter overview
Weight training progresses by adding load. Calisthenics also progresses by changing your body's leverage: shifting your position, removing support or holding longer. It is the same overload idea in a different form.
These ladders are a practical guide to the order of difficulty. Levlofit's level checks for calisthenics are set in the app.

## Why changing position works
> evidence: Evidence: moderate, from a small study
> In eight healthy men, a normal push-up put about 66.4% of body weight on the hands at the start, against 52.9% on the knees. Force and muscle activity changed significantly with hand position. {gouvali2005}
So a change of position really does change the work, without adding weight. The ACSM also concludes that many forms of resistance training work, which supports using bodyweight training as real training. {acsm2026}

## Movement ladders
Move up when the current step is under real control. Do not skip a step for variety.
| Chain | Easiest to hardest |
| Push | Incline push-up, push-up, decline push-up, archer push-up, pseudo-planche push-up |
| Pull | Negative pull-up, assisted pull-up, pull-up, archer pull-up |
| Row | Inverted row, feet-elevated inverted row |
| Dip | Bench dip, parallel-bar dip, ring dip |
| Squat | Bodyweight squat or split squat, assisted pistol squat, pistol squat |

## Hold ladders
Holds are timed in seconds. Start with a shorter lever or more support, and move to a longer lever or less support.
| Chain | Easiest to hardest |
| Handstand | Elevated pike push-up, wall handstand hold, wall handstand push-up |
| Front lever | Tuck front lever, advanced-tuck front lever |
| Core | Tuck L-sit, L-sit. Hollow-body hold, hollow-body rock. |
> warn: Advanced skills
> Wrists, elbows and shoulders can take a lot of load on advanced skills. Build up slowly, stop if something hurts, and get coaching for hard skills if you can.

## Range and mobility
If you train only with bodyweight, your lifting may not improve your range of motion the way loaded training can. {alizadeh2023} See [[mobility-flexibility|Mobility and flexibility]].

## Key takeaways
- Changing your position changes the work.
- Many forms of resistance training work, including bodyweight.
- Climb one step at a time, with control.
- Be careful with your joints on advanced skills.
